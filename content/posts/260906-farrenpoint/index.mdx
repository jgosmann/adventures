Like last year Marco planned a little hike for his birthday. This year, we
summit the Farrenpoint (1273m).

<Loc latlong={[47.74335278, 12.03555532]} />

After hiking about 2 hours on some nice trails, we reach the summit. Here we
enjoy the view and have some drinks that Marco stashed there the day before.
There is a bunch of cows around and some are quite curious.
