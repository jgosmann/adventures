The May starts with a long weekend and perfect weather. Sarah and I use it for
another multi-pitch at Leonhardstein: Fahrt ins Blaue (roughly “journey into the
unknown”, translated word-for-word “journey into the blue”).

<Loc latlong={[47.63704, 11.7161]} />

It takes us a while to get oriented at the cliff-base, but the route can be
clearly identified by the blueish bolt hangers. Sarah starts with the first
pitch <Grade system="UIAA" value="6" /> that starts a bit spicy, but frees her
from leading the second-pitch which is the crux pitch <Grade system="UIAA"
value="7" />. Instead I get the honour to lead it and manage to onsight it. The
crux is only a few short moves and didn't feel that much harder than the start
to me.

A few pitches higher, I'm leading again, is another interesting spot just after
a ledge. It takes me a while to figure out how to continue, but than it is
indeed not harder than the given grade <Grade system="UIAA" value="6" />.

The next pitch, I'm seconding, has another tricky spot. I have placed my feet
in the wrong position and fall out of the wall while trying to switch them
around. The second try with correctly placed feet works better.

Below the summit, we have a lunch break and enjoy the view. Then, after a quick
stop at the summit, we descend. On the way back to Munich, we grab some ice cream
at the Tegernsee to conclude a very enjoyable day of climbing.
