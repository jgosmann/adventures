The days are no longer as hot, and fall is around the corner. Now is the time
to squeeze in some last alpine tours before we get the first snow on the peaks.
Today, I'm planning to do the ridge line along the Geierköpfe.

<Loc latlong={[47.51917948, 10.87490112]} />

The bus drops me off at 9:30am. First, I follow a streambed littered with pebbles
before the trail starts to ascend through the woods. When the high trees turn
into mountain pines, I leave the officially marked trail, and fight my way
through the mountain pines towards the ridge line. Eventually, the thin trail
disappears. The GPS device reveals that I'm off track. But instead of going back
and trying to find a faint trail that agrees with the GPS track, I decide to
just go up the grassy slope. It is steep, but not so steep that it would be
difficult to go up. Once I reach the ridge line, I'm back on track and follow it
to the first summit, the eastern summit of the Geierköpfe (2060m).

After a short break, I continue to follow the ridge line towards the main summit
(2161m). Past a short descent, the first crux awaits. I have to stop for
a moment and study at the terrain to figure out that I have to downclimb a bit,
cross a slightly exposed groove, and finally climb up a few steps. In the end,ü it
looked more intimidating than it actually is. The guidebook gives two
difficulty ratings of <Grade system="UIAA" value="2" /> and <Grade system="UIAA"
value="3" />, but I think the latter one is a typo.

Not long after, another spot awaits me, where it is not immediately obvious how
to continue. Before me is a steep rock, to the right is a steep drop off, and to
the left we have a slope with loose gravel. Turns out, I have to go back
a couple of steps where there is a part descending a bit into the gravel slope,
going around the steep rock and than climbing back up to the ridge line behind
it.

Finally, there is another climbing spot (same unclear grading in Guidebook as
before, probably <Grade system="UIAA" value="2" />) that brings me right on top
of the main summit. There are already two other guys, but it is really not
a busy route. Apart from them, I just saw two trail runners and one other person
today.

With the most difficult parts done, I enjoy a slightly longer break. I'm a bit
worried that I might miss the last bus back. The guidebook gave a time estimate
of 7:30h and I have less than eight hours between the first bus (that I took)
and the last bus. But so far my speed is looking fine.

From the main summit to the western summit (2060), it is essentially hiking. The
first bit isn't a super easy hike as it involves quite a bit of loose, fine
gravel, but no more hands are required to hold on.

On the western summit, I have an even longer break before descending on
an official hiking trail. At one spot, I miss my trail branching off (apparently
that part is unsigned/unmarked again). But I quickly notice and just have to go
back a few hundred meters.

Eventually the Plansee lake comes back into view and the scenery look to me like
it could be in Canada. 😍

<Rimg src="14.jpeg" caption="Looks like it could be Canada, doesn't it?" />

In the end, I make it down quickly enough to catch the bus an hour earlier than
the last bus. Before that, I can even get a refreshing ice tea at the nearby
kiosk.

Without breaks, I took about 5:30h, much quicker than the guidebook time. My
watch says, I ascended 1700m which is considerably more than the 1200m stated in
the guidebook. With 1775m, I descended slightly more than I went up.
