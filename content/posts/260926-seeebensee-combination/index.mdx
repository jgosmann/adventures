This weekend, we've got beautiful sunny weather. While the mornings are already
quite cold, the day temperatures are still comfortably warm. I make use of the
weather with a combination of via ferratas all rated D/E.

<Loc latlong={[47.368511, 10.93505]} />

After a train and bus ride, I start at the valley station of the Ehrwalder
Almbahn quarter to 10am. From there, it is about 30 minutes along a, at this time
of day, quiet trail to the Seeben via ferrata. It takes me about 50 minutes and
I overtake a few other people. The via ferrata is quite nice because there
isn't an excessive amount of steel rungs; instead the easiest way up is often by
making good use of the actual rock. The via ferrata is sometimes also called
waterfall via ferrata, but there was no waterfall left at this time of year.

From the top of this first via ferrata, I pass the Seebensee and ascend towards
the Tajakanta via ferrata. It follows a ridge for over 650m of elevation up to
the Vordere Tajakopf (2450m). Easy terrain and steep, difficult spots alternate.
Before one such difficult spot, I overtake another person, who asks me whether
to climb it on the left or the right. An indication that he might not have had
much experience, but even worse, he didn't even have a proper via ferrata kit
and was just clipped in with some static cord.

In the upper part, I lose some time being stuck behind slower climbers. But
eventually I make it to the summit. I only take a short break; my timetable is
tight.

<Rimg src="16.jpeg" caption="View towards the Zugspitz massif." />
<Rimg src="17.jpeg" caption="View towards Drachenköpfe and Sonnenspitze." />
<Rimg src="18.jpeg" caption="Me on the summit." />

From here, I descend a little bit along an easy A/B via ferrata and some <Grade
system="UIAA" value="1-" /> climbing to a notch. After a little ascent back up,
the last via ferrata, the Coburger via ferrata, awaits. It starts with a short,
difficult stretch, and then quickly gets easier. It turns out to be quite short,
and then I'm already at the summit of the Hintere Tajakopf (2408m). Well, at
least I'm at the summit cross, where I have another short break. To the actual
summit, with only a small wooden cross, I have to walk a couple of meters over.
From there, I descend to the Drachensee and the nearby Corburger cabin. There
I get a drink before continuing my descent to the Seebensee. From the Seebensee,
I descent via the Hohe Gang, which has some more easy A/B via ferrata parts and
some loose, uncomfortable gravel patches. It brings me back to the bus stop with
half an hour to spare before the last bus.

On the train ride back, I still get some nice views of the mountains in the
distance as the sun is setting and the sky turns orange-violet above the
mountain silhouettes. At the same time, we have a full moon, appearing enormous
as it rises above the horizon. Especially passing the Lake Starnberg it is quite
beautiful with the light reflecting in the water and the mountains in the
distance.

The tour took me about 6h and had an elevation difference of about 1800m
according to me watch, but only 1500m according to the guide book.
