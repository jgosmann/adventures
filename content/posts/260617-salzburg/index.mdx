After a good breakfast, I’m back in the saddle. I have an early start because in
the early afternoon there might be thunderstorms. For now, I’m following the
Bodensee-Königssee cycling route, which [I already did last
year](/posts/250622-berchtesgaden/). The first twenty or thirty kilometers are
following a road. But eventually I turn onto a gravel path that I still remember
from last time. But it is shorter than I thought. At the end is a monastery and
an inn, where I have a radler and zander filet for lunch.

From here, a it goes mostly downhill until Piding. Here, I leave the
Bodensee-Königssee cycling route and veer off to Salzburg, following some more
nice paths through meadows with high grass and woodland. I eventually enter
Salzburg from the south along an old growth boulevard. Then suddenly, some rain
drops start falling, but luckily I’m close to a building that provides ample
space protected from the rain. There I wait it out for a few minutes. I then
continue but shortly after that it starts raining again. This time even heavier.
Luckily some trees still provide enough cover. After it stops again, I can
finally make it to my hotel without getting wet. The last couple hundred meters,
I push my bike on the sidewalk because it’s faster as the street is completely
clogged by cars.

After a shower, I get some food for dinner at a supermarket and take a stroll
towards the old city core. On a bench at the Schloss Mirabell, I eat my food
before continuing through the city core and up the castle, where I have a
nice view of the surroundings.
