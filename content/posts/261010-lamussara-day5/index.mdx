While the last few days have been relatively quiet at the refugi, today, while
we are having breakfast, a large group of hikers appears and orders breakfast of
their own. It is likely due to the weekend and the national holiday on Monday.

Today, we drive to Arbolí (technically not La Mussara anymore). After some easier
warm ups, we get onto Mighty Pennis <Grade system="french" value="6a+" />. The
start is already the crux, and the first bolt is quite high.
Luckily, there are two other climbers nearby with a clip stick that we use
gratefully. Close to the finish is a somewhat overhanging part on good holds,
but it takes me a bit to figure out the correct sequence, so I need a second try
to score the redpoint. Along the route, you get some great views of the mountain
village of Siurana.

Then, we move to another part of the wall. Among other routes, we do Moltes
Grasses <Grade system="french" value="6b" />. Elena can onsight it, and I flash
it afterwards. It is surprisingly easy for the area.

We then call it a day. Because we still have some time before dinner at 8pm, we
decide to drive to Siurana. It is also a well-known climbing area, but mostly
with much harder routes. But we don’t intend to climb there, but just want to
see the impressive rock formations and the beautiful village. Now, in the
evening, it is a bit less overrun than it probably is during the day, while the
warm and golden evening light makes everything look extra pretty.

As the sun is setting, we drive back to the refugi. For dinner we get good
portions of vegetable rice. While this could count as a filling meal in itself,
there is again a second course of a veggie burger bun with some antipasti-like
vegetable (eggplant and grilled pepper, I think). For desert, yoghurt and flan
are offered again, but in addition there is also chocolate cake that I decide
for.

The refugi is much more filled tonight (presumably due to the weekend and
because the upcoming Monday is the national holiday in Spain). Most are camping
on the refugi grounds, but we also got some additional people in our bunk beds.
