Today I’m starting a gravel cycling tour that will bring me through the
Salzburger Land and Hohe Tauern in a round trip from Munich back to Munich. At
9:40am, I am finished with my last minute preparations and start my ride towards
the east. On this first day, I'm following [a route that I did a couple of years
ago as day trip](/posts/230422-chiemsee/). The first few Kilometers, I am going
through the outskirts of Munich until I reach the Ebersberger Forst from where
I’m riding through the woods on pretty straight long gravel roads.

Past Ebersberg, I have a lunch break on a bench besides a small chapel. As
I continue, the cloud cover increases and every now and then a couple of rain
drops are falling for a while before stopping again. It is never so much that
I need to get out my rain gear. About sixty kilometers in, I reach Wasserburg am
Inn where I walk a bit through the old city core and get an ice cream.

Then I continue towards the Chiemsee. I remember the route to be bit nicer than
I experience it today. Many parts are along roads but finally, at the Eggstätter
See, a really nice gravel trail along the lake can be taken. Not much
longer until I reach Bernau, the destination for today.

because I have been at that ice cream shop before and know that it is quite
good.

The stats: 110km in 5:30h (w/o breaks), 20.1km/h average, 640m elevation gain.
