This long weekend, Sarah and I will be at the Petzl climbing festival in
Königstein in the Fraconian Jura. The Franconian Jura is well known for its
climbing. It is where the redpoint was invented and the world's first <Grade
system="french" value="9a" /> (Action Directe) was established.

At 11am, we leave Munich and drive North. About halfway, we stop in the Altmühltal at
the Mühltor crag to do a bit of climbing.

We start at a crack graded <Grade system="UIAA" value="5" />. I lead first and
it is fine in the beginning. But then the crack opens into an off-width. Too
wide to jam with your hand, but too narrow to climb it as a chimney. I can wedge
in my body side-ways, but that leaves almost no room to move. Any good holds are
deep inside the crack. It takes me quite a while and some rests in the rope to
build enough mental confidence to crawl upwards with a lot of effort. Probably
the hardest <Grade system="UIAA" value="5" /> I've ever done. Sarah climbs it
more as a corner and has less trouble, but it still doesn't seem super easy.

The next route has the same start (and even the same name), but branches off to
the left. It climbs much better and goes to a ledge from where there's another
short wall leading to the summit of the crag. As the topo isn't very precise,
I end up not climbing the actual continuation of the climb which would also be
graded <Grade system="UIAA" value="5" />, but the direct line graded <Grade
system="UIAA" value="6+" />.

The bolt distances in the Franconian Jura are known to be often quite far. Even
though, they aren't that far in this part of the route, they are farther apart
than I'd like with my current mental state. Luckily, I have some cams with me
that I can place and eventually I even load one to take a rest. It held!

After this rather scary introduction to climbing in this area, we continue the
drive to the festival. After finding a spot for Sarah's camper van and setting
up my tent, we explore the booths on the festival grounds and grab some dinner
at one of them.

In the evening, it starts to rain. So the official opening and screening of some
climbing movies is happening with everyone squished into the bad weather tent.
