To make use of another day with beautiful weather before it gets worse on
Sunday, I have planned a bike and hike tour. From the train station, I cycle up
about 600m of elevation to the Hirschsattel (“deer saddle”). Along the way,
I pass Schloss Hohenburg [where I was climbing a week
ago](/posts/260411-hohenburg). From the Hirschsattel, I continue on foot up to
the Ochsenkamp (1594m) and then along the ridge line to the Auerkamp (1607m).

<Pano2x
  src="1.jpeg"
  caption="View from the Ochsenkamp towards the Tegernsee."

The path to the Auerkamp has still quite some leftover snow from the winter and
one has to be careful. I then descend on the same way back to my bicycle.

<Pano2x src="2.jpeg" caption="View towards the Auerkamp." />

Next, I cycle to the Lenggrieser Hütte, a cabin where I have a deer goulash for
lunch. It is a substantial meal, but that is fine as not much more exertion
awaits me. After the meal I can just roll down on my bicycle to Lenggries. It is
fun and I'm back at the train station in no time.
