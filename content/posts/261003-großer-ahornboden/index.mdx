I've never been to the Große Ahornboden (“Great Maple Ground”) which is supposed
to be quite beautiful in the fall. So I decided to bike there from Lenggries and
back.

<GpxTrack src="20261003.gpx" />

First, I follow the Isar to the Sylvensteinspeicher. A route that I have cycled
multiple times already. The water level in the Sylvensteinspeicher is quite low
after a hot and dry summer. As soon as possible, I leave the road and use
a gravel path that I discovered when preparing the route. Eventually, I leave
the Isar, and turn more towards the South through the mountain forest in fall
colors.

After a while, the trail leads me back to the paved road that I follow from now
on. But here it has somewhat less traffic than at the Sylvensteinspeicher and
later turns into a toll road, reducing the traffic even more. Unfortunately,
I'm now in Austria and the forest gravel roads are prohibited for cyclists, so
I cannot use those. The road goes up, but not very steeply, so it is comfortable
to ride. The clouds from the morning have also disappeared and gave way to
the sun.

<Rimg src="7.jpeg" caption="The Rißbach." />

<Rimg src="8.jpeg" alt="Rißbach landscape." />

At a pasture with some trees turning colorful, I have a first break. Afterwards,
it is not much longer until I reach the Große Ahornboden with the yellow-leafed
trees at this time of year. It is not the vibrant red that I know from the
Indian Summer in Canada, but the landscape (also along the way) is still
beautiful.

Past the Große Ahornboden is a hotel and alp, where I have a drink and a light
meal (graved salmon). Then I cycle the same way in reverse back to Lenggries. At
the Sylvensteinspeicher, I even find a maple tree glowing with red leaves.

I covered a distance of 96.2km with 850m of elevation in a bit under five hours
(without breaks). My average speed was almost 20km/h with a maximum of
48.9km/h.
