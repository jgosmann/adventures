Breakfast at the refugi starts at 8:30am, later than the typical mountain hut in
the Alps. Afterwards, we walk to the La Mussara ruins and descend a nice trail
to the Les Campanilles crag.

When we arrive, it is still in the shade and
somewhat windy. Elena finds it too cold and initially just belays me for the
first few routes. For the first route, Sipegotimato <Grade system="UIAA"
value="5+" />, I need a second try to get the redpoint. On my first try, I climbed
too far left, but it turns out that the good holds are on the right. The second
route, Plena de Vida <Grade system="UIAA" value="5+" />, features a nice dihedral
and slab. After one more route and a few routes repeated by Elena, now that the
wall is slowly reached by the sun, I try Kagando Melodías <Grade system="french"
value="6b+" />. Today, my mental game is much more on point. The route has some
harder spots, but one is especially hard. You have to get over a band with
minimal holds. The solution, I find in the end, traverses to the right first,
where the band is a bit less high and you can pull up on some small crimps to
better holds. From there, I traverse back all the way to the left, where I’m
almost in the neighbouring Sipegotimato and can pull up the next band.
Afterwards, a few more small holds follow and in the end it goes up a ramp.
I manage to get the redpoint on my third try.

The final route of the day, Anda-Lui <Grade system="french" value="6b" />,
I have to hangdog. Quite early there is a spot, where I had to look around for
a long time how to get over another hold-less band. But in that spot, you could
stand without wasting much energy, allowing me to look around a long time, until
I figure out that you have a right side-pull and a high left foot, which then
allows you to reach up to the next hold above the band. But higher up, there is
another slightly protruding band without any holds where I have to sit in the
rope. In the end, the only solution I can find is to traverse to the left into
the neighbouring route and then back right. I don’t know whether that is the
intended solution, but anything else seemed impossible and by no means a <Grade
system="french" value="6b" />.

We ascend back to the refugi within in the sunset. For dinner, it is again
a salad, but slightly different with tuna this time, as first course. The second
course are potatoes with veggie burger buns. For desert, we get the choice of
yoghurt or flan (crème caramel), and we chose the latter.

<Rimg src="20.jpeg" alt="La Mussara sunset" />

<Rimg src="21.jpeg" alt="La Mussara ruins in the sunset" />

After dinner, we chat a bit with the hut keeper, who is a climber himself. He
bolted 400 routes and re-equipped 300 over 25 years. He gives us some
recommendations for sectors and climbing areas, but those are slightly above our
pay grade.
