Sarah had only the afternoon available, but we still wanted to get some rock
climbing in. Thus, we decided to attempt a short six-pitch route near Kufstein
without approach.

<Loc latlong={[47.596089, 12.193065]} />

Shortly past 3pm, we arrive. The route has two variants for the first pitch and
we decide for the harder <Grade system="UIAA" value="7+" /> variant. The first
part is easier, graded at <Grade system="UIAA" value="5+" />, but increases in
difficulty after that. Sarah leads the pitch, but falls around the crux and
bruises her hip. But eventually, she makes it to the top. However, it is the
wrong top. She should have traverse left to a different anchor a bit lower.

But now she cannot get there (without downclimbing, which is usually harder than
going up), so I follow her up. I also fall in the crux and have a hard time
getting back into the wall. Nevertheless, I also make it up eventually. We then
rappel and move over to the correct anchor for our route, which isn't so easy
either because it is not that much lower and quite a bit to the left. Once we
finally have made it there we decide that it is too late to do the rest of the
route. It is at most 90 minutes until it gets dark and we'd realistically need
at least two hours to complete the route. So, to not get deeper into trouble, we
rappel back to the ground and leave quite unaccomplished.
