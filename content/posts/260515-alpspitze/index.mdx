Temperatures of up to 34°C in Munich make me flee into the higher and cooler
elevations of the Alps. Especially the north-facing Mauerläufer via ferrata is
a good choice for such hot days, despite having it done
[three](/posts/190921-mauerlaeufer/) [times](/posts/210807-hoher-gaif/)
[already](/posts/250810-hoher-gaif/). I take the cable car up to the
Osterfelderkopf. From there it is a short walk to the start of the via ferrata.

<Rimg src="1.jpeg" caption="The north-face of the Bernadeinkopf." />

I climb it fast and efficiently, overtaking a few other people, and make it to
the summit of the Bernadeinkopf (2143m) until 11am. After a short break,
I continue via the south-east ridge line onto the summit of the Alpspitze
(2628m). I'm still quite quick and reach it shortly past 12pm.

After another break, I start descending towards the Grieskarscharte. It is nice
alpine terrain with a number of via ferrata parts. When doing the famous
Jubiläumsgrat, one option is to go up here to the Alpspitze at the end.
Another option is to descent through the Grieskar to my left. Straight ahead
starts the Jubiläumsgrat, marked with a warning sign. But today, I continue my
descend to the right into the Mathaisenkar. It is quite a long easy, but alpine,
via ferrata descent until I pass across a scree field and hit a normal hiking
trail that brings me to the Höllentalangerhütte.

A bit before the Höllentalangerhütte, a helicopter can be heard. It hovers for
quite a long time near [the Brett of the Zugspitze
ascent](/posts/200731-zugspitze/). Then, it flies a bit higher, closer to the
Höllentalferner, before turning around and flying through the Höllental, not far
from me. It must have been some mountain rescue operation.

At the Höllentalangerhütte, I have a drink (an elder flower spritzer, which
advanced to one of my favourite re-hydration drinks on hot days this summer) and
an apple strudel. Afterwards, the descend continues to Hammersbach. However, this time
I don't go through the Höllentalklamm, but take the Stangensteig that crosses
the Höllentalklamm at a height of 70m and is not less impressive.

I was expecting the trains to run always quarter past, but didn't have
sufficient cell phone reception. I wanted to catch the train at 5:15pm, so I had
to keep my pace. The times given on the signage was quite tight. But in the end,
I was much faster than the given times, and also it turned out that the trains
run at 33 minutes past the full hour. So I almost, but not quite make the train
4:33pm. My first thought is, that I have to wait for an hour and could have gone
down much slower. Luckily, the trail spits me out right at a bus stop with
a huge crowd. Turns out, there is also a bus supposed to run at 4:34pm. It is
a couple of minutes late and I can catch it. Good timing!

Unfortunately, there is construction on the train tracks between Tutzing and
Munich. Thus, I have to change into the S-Bahn in Tutzing. Already there it is
quite full, but it gets extremely crowded in Starnberg, where always many day
tourists want to go back to Munich when the weather is nice. As I expected,
this leads to a medical emergency in the train, so that we accumulate a 30
minute delay while waiting for the doctor. But at least, the change in Tutzing
was just across the opposite track, unlike the main station where you always
have to walk quite the way, and the S-Bahn brings me close to home without any
other change.

This tour took me about 6h, with 1600m of elevation gain and 2750m of elevation
loss.
