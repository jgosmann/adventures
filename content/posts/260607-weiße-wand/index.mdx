It is the last day of the festival. We attend another yoga session, today at
9am. Then we pack up everything and drive to the Weiße Wand (“white wall”) crag.
The weather is now finally stable and quite sunny, but also quite windy. All in
all this gives excellent temperatures for climbing.

We climb several nice, albeit sometimes polished routes. In Chrysalis <Grade
system="UIAA" value="6-" />, we place a cam even before the first bolt. It
isn't extremely high for Franconian standards, but the few moves to reach it
feel a bit insecure. At the very least it calms the mind.

A while later, two young girls from a youth group of the alpine club also try
it. The one leading apparently also felt insecure on it and wants to down climb.
But then the disaster happens. I only see it from the corner of my eyes, but she
falls out of the wall, tilting with the upper body downwards and slamming face
first into the rock on the ground. For a moment everyone is frozen in shock. It
is unclear how severe the injuries are. But then the girl moves and it becomes
clear that the injuries are not life threatening. A tooth has been knocked out,
and the right hands looks a bit swollen. The girl itself is surprisingly
composed.

After everyone is calmed down, the bleeding of the tooth hole stopped, and the
arm with the swollen hand fixated, she can descend by herself with her group and
be driven to a hospital.

For our next route, Weiss der Geier <Grade system="UIAA" value="7" />, with a not
so easy start, we decide to ask around for stick clip after this experience (we
had forgotten our one at the car). Turns out, the youth group had one that it
lends to us. Might have been a good idea if they had used it themselves too.

After an enjoyable, but quite powerful <Grade system="UIAA" value="6" /> named
Bauchtanz (“belly dance”), we call it a day, and drive back to Munich.
