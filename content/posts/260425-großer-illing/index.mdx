Today I'm doing a multi-pitch route at the Southwest arête of the Großer Illing
(1313m) together with Fabian.

<Loc latlong={[47.624011, 11.263784]} />

After a nice approach, we encounter the route much busier than we expected.
Before us are already two other teams. Thus, we have to wait for a while. Before
we start, another pair of climbers arrives to enter the route after us.

<Gallery>
  <Rimg src="1.jpeg" caption="The start of the route." />
  <Rimg src="2.jpeg" caption="View in the middle of the route." />
</Gallery>

We take alternating leads. As Fabian did the route before, I get to lead the
more interesting and engaging pitches. After a lunch break on the summit, we
descend back to Ohlstadt to take the train back to Munich.
