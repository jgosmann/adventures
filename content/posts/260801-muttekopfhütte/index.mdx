Sarah, Anna, Marco, and I are planning to stay a couple of nights at the
Muttekopfhütte for some climbing as we [enjoyed it last
year](/posts/250606-muttekopfhuette/). Fabian and his girlfriend were supposed
to be part of it too, but couldn’t join due to sickness unfortunately.

<Loc latlong={[47.262448, 10.669753]} />

Sarah and I start with her camper in the morning towards Imst. There is quite
a lot of traffic on the streets. Eventually, we realize it is the beginning of
the school holidays in Bavaria, so many people are heading towards the south.
Maybe not the best planning on our side because we are not tied to the vacation
times. In addition, we find out that there is a protest on the direct route
against too much traffic (which I can understand) and we have to take detour.

In the end, these delays turn out to be quite fortunate. Shortly before Imst, we
are hit by a short thunderstorm and downpour. We are quite happy to not be
hiking in this weather. But as we arrive at the parking lot (where we meet
Marco), the weather is looking fine again and should stay dry for the next
couple of hours.

For various reasons, we decide to take the cable car up instead of walking. From
the summit station, we follow the sign for Muttekopfhütte, saying 2h of walking.
It goes uphill and a short while later we are at the summit of the Vorderes
Alpjoch (2121m), where we realize that this is the long way around and not the
direct route we intended to take. After studying the map, we descent back to the
summit station. There another, a bit less obvious, a bit exposed trail takes us
to the cabin within 30 minutes.

At the cabin, we leave some gear and walk with just our climbing stuff to the
Guggerköpfle for a few easy sport climbing routes. A while later, also Anna
joins us, who came up independently.

Just in time for dinner at 6pm, we walk back to the cabin. After dinner, we
check-in, prepare our bunk beds, discuss plans for the next day, and go to sleep
early.
