The last two days, I attended the conference and didn't do any sightseeing.
Today is my last full day in Barcelona. In the morning, I visit Park Güell, where
Antoni Gaudí also left his marks. I also visit the Gaudí House Museum there.
While the park is nice, it is also quite overrun with tourists. Not sure if it
is even more so today because it is a weekend.
