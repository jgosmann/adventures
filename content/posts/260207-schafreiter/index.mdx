Today I'm joining a snow shoeing tour organized by the German Alpine Club (DAV).
We were supposed to be six including our guide, but the sixth person doesn't
show up and so we eventually leave Munich as a group of five.

<Loc latlong={[47.50885, 11.48758]} />

Between Vorderriß and Hinterriß, we start our hike. First we follow a gravel
road. Depending on how much sun a particular spot gets, it alternates between
being covered with a thin layer of hard snow (or ice) and being snow-free.

After about 400m of elevation (about a third of the total elevation gain today),
the gravel road ends and the snow gets deeper. As it is cloudy, we don't get
much of a view. But for the final few meters we get a good view of the summit
at 2102m.

clouds suddenly break away and we finally get a nice panorama.

In the sun, we descend more or less on the ascension path. However, we veer
a bit more into the mountain pines. This somewhat treacherous terrain as they
can create pockets under the snow cover in which a foot may suddenly disappear.
Exactly this happens to me and the snow shoes gets caught under the mountain
pine so that I cannot free it without taking of the snow shoe, getting out of
the hole, and then retrieving the snow shoe with my hands.

After about 5h, we are back at our starting point. Thus, we were quite fast for
the 1200m of elevation.
