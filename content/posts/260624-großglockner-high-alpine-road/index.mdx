Breakfast is served at 7:30am and I manage to start cycling at 8:10am. The
ascent along the Großglocker high alpine road starts immediately.
Surprisingly, I manage to catch up with a woman on a racing bike and no luggage
until the toll gate. She'll overtake me again at my first break at the Kasereck
after about an hour in. Here I did already about 700m of elevation (of 1660m on
total today). So far it has been going much better than expected. The scenery is
fantastic and traffic is still little this early. At this height, the temperature
is also pretty comfortable.

Now, a short downhill segment awaits before the second part of the ascent. As
I turn onto it, I get hit by head winds. But at the next switch back, the wind
comes from behind and pushes me up the hill. Unfortunately, most of the time,
after one more switch back, it will be head or side winds again.

I'm occasionally overtaken by cyclists on lighter bikes, but they aren't
that much faster (though it will add up to quite a bit over the full distance).
Also the group of the other four cyclists that stayed in the same hotel passes
me one after another.

Shortly before the Wallackhaus, another 400m of elevation higher and 1:45h in,
I overtake the woman from earlier again, before she passes me once more when
I take another break. Now it is the final 200m of elevation to the Hochtor at
2504m, the official pass and highest point of of the high alpine road, but not
the end of climbing for today. Though first, I meet the group of four cyclists
from my hotel again.

I then continue through a tunnel that leads through the ridge line. It follows
a nice downhill ride of about 250m of elevation and one more climb. I then take
a branch off of the high alpine road to climb another 170m on cobblestones to the
summit of the Edelweißspitze. It ends at 2571m, the highest point in my
vacation. Though, I find it a bit questionable that you can drive up here with
cars and motorcycles.

Anyways, I have lunch at the summit's cabin
(Kaspressknödelsuppe) and spend quite some time enjoying the view. Today's hotel
allows check-in again only past 4pm and it should take me only about an hour to
get there. So I have lots of time up here and the temperature is much nicer than
the 30 degrees Celsius in the valley.

Eventually, I start the descent, but stop at two small exhibitions about the
alpine nature and mountains. At the Schleierwasserfall I readjust my break pads.
All the downhill braking (many switch backs where you have to go slow) is
definitely causing some faster wear on the break pads.

<Gallery>
  <Rimg src="21.jpeg" caption="Schleierwasserfall" />

Near Fusch, the slope flattens out a bit, but still goes downhill. However,
I also have suddenly strong head winds and need to pedal quite a bit despite the
slope.

A few more kilometers and I make it to my hotel at the Zeller See. Same
procedure as most evenings: taking a shower and getting dinner. I have Pinzgauer
Kasnocken, a local speciality similar to Käsespätzle (the Austrian and better
version of mac‘n‘cheese).

<Pano2x src="10.jpeg" caption="Zeller See late in the evening." />

The stats: 58.8km in 4h (w/o breaks), 14.8km/h average, 1660m elevation gain.
