Sarah and I use a warm spring Saturday for some sport climbing at Hohenburg. It
is our first time at the crag and at first we have a bit of trouble to find the
thin approach trail hidden under the leaves from last fall. The guide's
description and map are also not really helpful.

<Loc latlong={[47.673537, 11.589873]} />

It is not a large crag, but we find that it has a few nice routes, though
without any official names. After a warm up we get onto <Grade
system="UIAA" value="6+/7-" /> within a corner. It has a nice crag where the
walls meet, but the bottom parts seems a bit unstable. It is also the crux of
the route. I need a few attempts to make it over this bit, but then I can enjoy
the rest of the route without falling. It is engaging climbing with a final
slightly tricky part right before the anchor. Sarah tries it next, but an
essential hold at the start breaks as she pulls on it. The route might be harder
now. We don't know for sure. Being a bit intimidated by the rock quality, she
now uses the first draw to pull herself up to the second one and climb the
remainder of the route.

Afterwards we try a variant of our warm-up that is supposed to be <Grade
system="UIAA" value="8-" />, but we cannot figure out the beta. We finish on
a <Grade system="UIAA" value="6" />, which seems about a grade harder, before
calling it a day mid-afternoon as Sarah had to get back to Munich for a birthday
party.
