Because of excellent weather, I spontaneously took a vacation day to do some
multi-pitch climbing with Elena at the Leonhardstein (1449m).

<Loc latlong={[47.63704, 11.7161]} />

From the train station in Tegernsee, we cycle to Kreuth and ascend from
there. After a while, the approach trail gets quite narrow and leads through
a dense, mossy forest with some leftover patches of snow. It is a quite nice
atmosphere. It is not suitable for bicycles like on the approach I took [a few
years back](/posts/220807-fuer-andi-leonhardstein/), but definitely the nicer
trail when you're on foot.

<Rimg src="1.jpeg" caption="The approach." />

We decide to climb Flora Bohra <Grade system="UIAA" value="6/6+" /> today.
Probably the most popular route here, but given that is a Friday, we are the
first on it. We have some trouble finding the correct start and end up starting
on Dreiplattenweg. This makes the start a bit harder, but I still climb it
without problems. After the first two pitches (that I combine into one), Elena
crosses over into the correct route that we climb to the top with nice views and
sunny weather.

<Rimg src="2.jpeg" caption="The summit." />

After a break and enjoying the view of the summit, we descend back to our
bicycles and make our way back to Tegernsee where we catch the train back with
a minute to spare.
