I barely slept this night. Nevertheless, I get up quarter past seven to be ready
for yoga at eight. It is happening in the bad weather tent because it rained
during the night.

We spent the morning waiting for things to dry off and listen to two talks at
the festival stage about how to deal with fear in climbing and how to create a
training plan. There were little concrete things to take away, though. During
the second talk I also dozed off given my sleep deficit and comfortable deck
chairs.

At the end of the second talk, it starts to rain again. Thus, we decide to walk
to village center and get some food at the super market. Back at the
camper and tent, we have some lunch. It has been dry again for a while now, so
we decide to finally go climbing, but just in that moment it starts raining
again.

We kill some more time on the festival grounds. With the repeated showers, we
are unsure whether it is still worth going climbing. But past 4pm we say
“f\*\*\* it”, get our stuff and walk to the Breitenstein. This time it stays dry
and there is even dry rock. But of course it is busy there and every route that
isn't too hard is already taken. We decide to walk a bit farther to the
Steinberg and indeed there are some free routes. As it is rather late everyone
else leaves after a while, so that we can climb by ourselves until about 7pm.
Many of the routes feature pockets which are quite typical for the region.

<Loc latlong={[49.604223, 11.651755]} zoom={15} />

We then walk back to the campsite, where we throw together some food items and
make some pasta with lentils, feta cheese, and spices.

Afterwards, we visit an interesting talk about big wall climbing in Yosemite by
Lara Neumeier. Next, the sons of the famous German mountaineer and rock climber
[Thomas Huber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Huber) play some live rock
music, followed by Plastic Surgery Disaster, Thomas Huber's own rock band.
Unfortunately, I have to say his climbing is better than his vocals. As it's
getting quite cold and I'm tired from the previous sleepless night, I don't stay
until the end.
