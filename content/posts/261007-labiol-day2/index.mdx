In the night it rained, but it stopped and is slowly drying off. So there is
nothing left standing between us and our first climbing day. After breakfast and
a short drive, we start the approach to the Lo Soterrani crag.

<Loc latlong={[41.241735, 1.071349]} zoom={14} />

However, we didn’t yet discover the page with the approach descriptions in the
guide book and end up descending too much. So we have to hike back up for a bit.
Eventually, we find a narrow trail that seems to lead to the cliffs and Elena
decides to follow it ambitiously. Soon after, we are fighting through thorny
bushes. But we prevail and end up at the cliff, only for Elena to notice that
she lost her sunglasses in there. So she has to back down and back up again to
retrieve them.

We start with some easy routes in the <Grade system="UIAA" value="5" /> range,
slowly increasing the difficulty to get a feel for the area's grading.
Supposedly, it is rather on the stiffer side. Eventually, we want to try
Elegosentrik <Grade system="french" value="6a+" />. It turns out to be quite
hard. Elena sits multiple times in the rope. When it is my turn, after she put up
the draws, I also have to hangdog it. I’m also mentally struggling (maybe
because the unexpected difficulty, maybe because I haven’t been climbing outside
in a while, or maybe for some other unrelated reasons). One or two draws from
the top, with the next bolt in reach, I am unable to clip at first and even have
a mild panic attack despite consciously knowing that a fall would be perfectly
save (it’s slightly overhanging with a clear fall line). But then my foot finds
something to stand on (due to a bulge not visible from the top), and I can do
the clip. Later we figure out, that we didn’t climb Elegosentrik, but some
other route that we cannot unambiguously identify. The only route left of it
listed in online resources is a <Grade system="french" value="6c" />, but it
should be a trad line, while we climbed a sport route.

After this experience, we climb with The Talker Bom <Grade system="french"
value="6a" /> one more route, which unfortunately has holds which are quite
painful for me and doesn’t make me any happier.

On the way out, we find the correct trail this time. It is already dusk as we
drive back to our accommodation. On the way, a quite large, dark boar passes our
way.

For dinner we get pasta with vegetables and soy minced “meat”.
