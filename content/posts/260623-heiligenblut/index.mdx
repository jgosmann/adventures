I‘m starting to notice the toll of the last week. I feel my leg muscles
and the neck is hurting a bit. At least distance-wise it is a short day, but
still quite a few meters of elevation (though also less than the last days).

I continue along the Drau for a bit, and the mountains start to get a bit more
rocky with high cliffs. Eventually, I turn north and start the ascent to
Heiligenblut. After a steep ascent, I reach a saddle and have a short downhill
ride. Then, the track follows the Möll stream and ascents slowly towards
Heiligenblut. I feel a bit tired, maybe also because of the heat, even though it
is a bit cooler being at a higher elevation now.

I reach the hotel by 1:30pm, but cannot check in yet. So I wander up to the
village center where I have a potato salad and a drink to rehydrate. Afterwards,
I relax on a shady bench a bit outside the village. I need to recover as much as
possible for tomorrow, when I am going across the main alpine ridge again on the
(in)famous Großglockner high alpine road. I'm a bit worried that I might not
make the steep and long ascent. I'm also thinking about ditching the mountain
route on the day after tomorrow, going through the valley instead.

At 4pm, I can check in at the hotel. There arrived also a group of four cyclists,
but with racing bikes and minimal luggage, and a bunch of motorcyclists. You can
tell quite clearly who's who by the build of the body.

After a shower, I go for dinner and have a pizza. Then, I prepare everything for
the next day and go to bed early to be able to start as early as possible.

The stats: 53.1km in 3:30h (w/o breaks), 15.1km/h average, 1100m elevation gain.
