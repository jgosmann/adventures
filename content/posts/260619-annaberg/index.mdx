Breakfast isn't served before 8am today, so I'm not starting quite as early as
I would've liked given the hot day it is going to be. But at 9am, I'm back in the
saddle. It doesn't take long before the ascent towards the Postalm awaits.
First, it is along a stream and enjoyably shady. Nevertheless, it is already
quite warm and I'm sweating like hell. On the way, I pass the start of the
Postalm via feratta, which [I have done before](/posts/220913-postalmklamm/).
After an hour, I take a break of a few minutes under some trees to cool off. The
trees are getting less and the sunny stretches are getting longer. However,
there is a horse fly that considers me tasty and is getting quite annoying. So
I continue and after another 20 minutes I make it to the Postalm. There I have
a Radler to refill liquids lost through sweating. At the nearby chapel is a mass
with some trumpet music.

When I continue, it is just a few more meters of elevation and then a long
downhill ride through splendid mountains awaits. It is again following
a mountain stream. After a while, I am stopped by signage that the road ahead
is closed due to forest works. But people are coming the other way, so
apparently you can go through and the alternative would be to cycle quite
a distance back uphill. So I try my luck and don't find any evidence of actual
forest works.

Close to the end of the downhill section, I stop and have a lunch break near the
stream. I purposefully prolong it to stay a bit more in the shade during the
hour that the sun is scorching down most intensely. Downhill the wind chill
makes it comfortable, but another climb will await me.

But before the next climb, I have to ride a bit through the valley, but also get
some nice views of the Dachstein here. A few kilometers are on a main road
without cycle paths. These are not as nice, though the cars keep good distance
when overtaking except for that one idiot that you always get. Then I veer off
the main road onto a quieter road up to a ski region. Getting to the “village”
of my final destination, it feels a bit eerie with lots of nice vacation
houses, but no person to be seen. Anyway, the hotel allows me to check-in
already shortly before 3am. As I spend the rest of the afternoon showering and
planning the next day, at least a bit more guests appear around the hotel.
I suppose, during winter time it is super crowded here and hotel prices are
probably much higher.
