It's the first mountain stage of the tour today. After a good breakfast, I leave
Salzburg along the Salzach towards the south. After a few kilometers, the first
uphill section starts. It goes through a cool, shady forest which is quite
welcome as the sun is already burning. Eventually, I overtake a woman with
a bicycle trailer. As far as I can tell, she is not riding an e-bike and gains
my respect for that. Three to four hundred meters of elevation higher, the grade
turns flatter and the track goes through beautiful hilly meadows until the first
downhill ride awaits. It’s good fun.

But when you’re all the way down, the next ascent awaits. This time it goes
along the impressive Strubklamm, a deep canyon. At the Felsenbad, a rock
structure with many small pools, I stop and enjoy the nature for a while. Today
I have enough time and don’t need to be stressed.

The next part isn't as steep, but still a bit inclined. It passes some pretty
lakes before spitting me out onto a paved road. But eventually I leave the paved
road and turn onto a steep gravel road that brings me above 1000m of elevation.
Then I get a great downhill ride before another ascent to the Lärchenhütte. It
is almost the highest point today. I definitely earned my lunch there (homemade
ice tea, a Kaspressknödel-burger, and yoghurt from sheep‘s milk for dessert;
didn’t taste much different from normal yoghurt, though). It feels really good
enjoying the meal, view, and sunny weather, knowing that I made it up here by
fair means (unlike the good number of e-bikers).

When I continue, I have to gain a little bit more elevation, before I can
roll downhill. The first part of the downhill is surprisingly difficult. It’s
quite rocky, but also lots of loose rocks, in parts even muddy, and quite steep.
Here I have to go slow, as I don’t want to injure myself. I’m quite happy that
my bike has a dropper post (a feature that I thought of a gimmick at first). It
allows me to get my center of gravity a bit lower and my body backwards to stay
centered over the wheels, despite the steep drop, to have better control over the
bike.

Eventually, the rough terrain turns into a normal gravel road and then even into
a paved road. But before that, a boom gate is blocking my way. It is locked and
there is no way around it that fits my bicycle. With some effort and tilting my
bicycle quite sideways, I would be able get through below. But luckily a car
arrives from the other side in just that moment, and the driver happens to have
the key for the gate. Soon I'm in the valley at the Wolfgangsee and ride along it
towards Strobl my final stop. On the way, I shortly stop at an observation deck
overlooking the swamp bordering the lake. At a supermarket, I also get food for
dinner.

At the hotel, I do a bit of bike maintenance (lubricating the chain, adjusting
the break pads) before showering and dining on the balcony with view of the
mountains.
