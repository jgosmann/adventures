Today, I'm crossing the main alpine ridge line. This will be a strenuous day and
unfortunately I'm not feeling 100% fit, but still fit enough to believe it's
possible. But I'm definitely happy that I don't have to do the extra elevation
of the loop yesterday. I can comfortably follow the Enns to Altenmarkt to warm
up a bit, before turning south, where I start to slowly ascent. The path follows
the Taurach stream and is quite nice. Shortly past Untertauern, it ends and
I have to continue on the federal highway. The landscape is absolutely stunning,
but with cars and motorcycles sometimes passing quite closely, it is hard to
enjoy.

physically taxing, but at least no threat of cars. For a short stretch I have to
get back on the road before turning towards the Gandenalm where I get more
gravel trails without cars.

After enjoying the view, maybe the most challenging part awaits: a short stretch
is a rocky hiking trail that definitely cannot be cycled. A mountain biker
coming the other way is telling me exactly this (which already know, being
prepared) and that it would've been better to take the road. Anyways, I push and
carry up my bike first, then the luggage. A bit more of quite rugged, but for the
most part cycleable trail follows before hitting a paved road again. It still
goes uphill, but on the pavement it goes easier. Until I unexpectedly hit the
next segment where I need to push my bike. But I also make it through that and
eventually join back with the federal road in Obertauern for the last few meters
of elevation to the highest point of the pass at 1739m. It is again a ski resort
and the village is pretty dead.

A fast paced descent to Tweng follows. It is still on the federal road, but
going downhill fast (and maybe because the time of the day) I'm not overtaken by
that many cars. Unlike going uphill, there also isn't a danger that I might
suddenly shimmy. The road isn't very curvy and quite wide, so I barely have to
brake.

In Tweng, I order a drink at the first inn I come along to rehydrate before
continuing to Mauterndorf, now away from the street on gravel trails going up
and down. In Mauterndorf, I take a look at the castle, but don’t have time to
visit it.

Another long ascent awaits. At least it is not as steep as some of the others,
but still demanding given the all the elevation gain earlier in the day. At
Grubental (1538m), I think I made it. But then I notice there's a turn onto
another steep single track trail that I need to push my bike up. Sigh. But at
the summit of the Hollerberg (1601m), I finally made it. Now it’s just rolling
downhill. Midway, I stop to pull out my rain jacket because cloud cover has
formed and increasingly more raindrops are falling. But this works
perfectly, and by the time I put the jacket on, it has already stopped. Given
the windchill rolling down, it is still fine to keep it on.
