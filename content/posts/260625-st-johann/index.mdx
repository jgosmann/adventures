a very important historical site as a sign on a tree says that there happened
absolutely nothing on the 14th of October 1779.

At the border of Saalfelden, I have the option to take the route through the
valley or do another mountain day as originally planned. Despite the heat,
I decide for the latter. At least it will offer a bit more shade and it will be
cooler at the higher altitude in the afternoon. The first part of the ascent is
about 700m of elevation and brings me to the Biberghaus. It is quite steep and
Komoot warned me that 200m are so steep that by I will have to push my bike.
Somehow I manage without pushing my bike, but with some stops in-between. At the
Biberghaus, I rehydrate with a non-alcoholic wheat bear and have a light lunch
(salad with Kaspressknödel). I can watch the pretty panorama while eating.

The route continues with a short downhill ride and another major climb of 400m
of elevation, offering great views. Below the Schönleitenhütte, I decide to also
go up the additional 40m of elevation to it, in hope of a cool drink, but it
seems to be closed. So I have to continue. It is another two short downhill
segments, each followed by a short ascent. On the way, I pass some more alps,
but they are also closed; probably only open in the winter for ski tourists.
Finally, I get to an alp that is open, advertises après-bike as there are quite
a few mountain bike tracks around here, and blasts Schlager music. Not the most
welcoming atmosphere, but at least I can get a drink (elder soda) and a bite to
eat (apple strudel). Turns out, they are also a bit pricey compared to other
locations and mountain cabins. They also serve beer and Radler as 0.4l sizes
instead of the usual 0.5l while not charging any less (rather more). So wouldn't
recommend, when not in dire need of liquid and calories like me.

From here follows the last long downhill ride of the vacation. Then it is
another 20km in the heat of the valley to St. Johann. Mostly going flat or
slightly downhill, but every now and then a short incline. Sometimes, I also get
some strong-ish head winds, but at least they cool me a bit.

In St. Johann, I follow once more the usual evening program: shower, dinner
(pasta, tiramisu), ice cream as second desert (I had better), and a bit of relaxing.

The stats: 65.9km in 5:30h (w/o breaks), 12.2km/h average, 1760m elevation gain.
