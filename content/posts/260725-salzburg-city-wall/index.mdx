Today, I'm in Salzburg to do the City Wall via ferrata. It is located in the
heart of Salzburg.

<Loc latlong={[47.804099, 13.04976]} />

There are a few variants. I start with the “Kapuziner Gams” variant with
difficulty rating E. It starts steep and the rock is quite polished. After a D/E
traverse, it joins up with the easier variant, before splitting off again for
the second part with the E crux. The middle part used by both variants is
extremely polished and slippery, but still doable. As the rock face is only
fifty meters high, I'm finished with it in about thirty minutes.

I then walk back down, the last bit through a part of the old Salzburg city
core, where I'm a bit out of place with my via ferrata equipment. Next, I try
the easier C variant “Bergspezl Route”. The upper part is quite nice with
a wobbly ladder that gives a great view of Salzburg and some exposed bits.
This is my favourite part of the via ferrata.

<Gallery>
  <Rimg src="4.jpeg" caption="The wobbly ladder." />
  <Rimg src="3.jpeg" caption="Looking down." />
</Gallery>

I walk down once more. This time, I start again with the hard “Kapuziner Gams”,
but in the middle, I take the last variant “Salewa Route” with difficulty B.
At the top, I climb it back down and then do another lap of the upper part of
“Kapuziner Gams”. Then I downclimb the “Salewa Route” once more, to do the nice
upper part of the “Bergspezl Route” one final time.

Having done each variant at least twice, I stow away my via ferrata gear. After
enjoying the view for a while, I ascent the summit of the small Kapuziner Berg
(636m). There is a small castle on it. On the way down, I pass a nice view
point overlooking the Salzburg castle.

I walk back down, and get a sandwich for lunch at a café. They also have quite
tasty looking cinnamon buns. I can't resist and also get one those (with banana
topping).

<Rimg src="7.jpeg" alt="Cinnamon bun" />

I still have some time, so I visit the cathedral. [When I passed through Salzburg
during my cycling tour](/posts/260617-salzburg/), I couldn't visit it because
after checking in and showering at the hotel, it was already closed.

Then, I make my way back to the train station and go back to Munich.
