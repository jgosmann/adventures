Mediterranean Sea. The parking lot is right at a beach. But we walk some nice
trails through a nature preserve for about half an hour to get to a more beautiful,
secluded beach to take our swim.

Then, we walk back to the car, and drive the remaining distance back to the
airport, where we return the rental car. Elena will head home, so it is time to
say goodbye now.

I will stay for another week in Barcelona. Thus, I take the metro to the city
center and check-in into my hotel. Then, I head to the modern art museum Moco,
which is opened until late in the evening. They have works of Banksy, Kurusama,
and more.

Afterwards, I grab dinner and head back to the hotel. On the way, I pass
Basílica de Santa Maria del Mar and take a look inside.
