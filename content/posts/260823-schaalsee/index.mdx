I'm visiting Martin and Kristina this weekend. They are currently living near
Mölln for a few months. The region has quite a few lakes and we are planning
a kayak tour today.

We rent two kayaks at a campsite near the Schaalsee. The original plan was to
paddle through parts of the Schaalsee, but an additional permit is required
because it is a nature reserve. Thus, we change plans to paddle the other
direction, which is supposed to be also quite beautiful and features more
variety with two smaller lakes and some channels.

<Loc latlong={[53.639973, 10.858827]} />

Martin and I start with the kayaks, while Kristina cycles with their two kids in
the trailer. At some of the lake beaches, we meet up shortly. Then, we
start paddling through the channel. At first it goes through a nice forest,
often with branches reaching into the water, sometimes creating a bit of an
obstacle parcours. The scenery is very serene. Eventually, the forest clears and
the channel gets a bit more boring for the last part. But worse, it starts to
rain and we have to get out our rain jackets. At first the rain isn't too heavy,
but then it gets much more. Luckily, we are close to a bridge under which we
find protection, wait out the rain, and eat a bit.
