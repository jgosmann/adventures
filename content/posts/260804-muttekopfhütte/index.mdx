Today is our departure day. Anna has to leave right after the breakfast. Marco,
Sarah and I do some more sport climbing at the sector Eisenhut until noon. It is
a bit difficult to figure out which bolt line is which route, with conflicting
information on the lines in our two guidebooks.

<Loc latlong={[47.263529, 10.66629]} zoom={16} />

After three climbs, we descend back to the cabin, collect our remaining luggage
and walk down to Imst. We then drive back to Munich after a nice prolonged
weekend of climbing.
