Again, breakfast (with the smallest bread rolls I have ever seen) isn't served
before 8am, so it is not the earliest start shortly past 9am. It directly starts
with a climb of a few hundred meters of elevation and then a short downhill
section before the long climb of the day. At the beginning, it has a very steep
segment that I'm barely able to ride (but I manage). An old man on an e-bike has
the audacity to overtake me with a ring of his bell. But also that climb comes
to an end and a long and nice downhill ride awaits. Suddenly, a male deer jumps
from the left to the right across my path. I didn't hit it or even had to brake
to hard, but this is a nice addition to the lizard and multiple cats that I see
today.

Down from the mountain, the route follows a road, slowly descending through the
valley. Despite head winds, I don't have to pedal too hard to make good
progress. After another section that just adds another loop and some meters of
elevation, I make it to Flachau. Here I’ll be staying for the night.

<Pano src="1.jpeg" alt="View approaching Flachau." />

But it's just around noon and the thunderstorms that might happen in the
afternoon are still out of sight. So after a lunch break, I tackle the first
section of tomorrow's route. It's another 400m of elevation to the Sattelbauer
inn. Unfortunately, the way up is less shady than I hoped for. A couple of
times I stop for a few minutes to cool down and catch my breath.

<Rimg src="8.jpeg" alt="View of the mountains." />

At the inn, I have an isotonic drink made from elder blossoms and local honey to
rehydrate and I eat second lunch (potato strudel). Another man, looking for
a spot in the shade, joins me at the table and we chit chat a bit about our
vacations.

Now it’s almost time for the last downhill ride of the day, but there are a few
more meters of elevation before it. This time they are too steep and I have to
push my bike up a little bit. The downhill ride is delightful. In the end,
I follow the Enns (a small stream) which brings me back to Flachau, where I can
check-in into my hotel and get some food for dinner from the nearby supermarket.

Tomorrow, I will ride back along the Enns to meet my route again and save myself
from the elevation that I already did today. It will be a strenuous day even
without that part as I will be crossing the main alpine ridge line. For that,
I also lubricate my chain again and readjust my brake pads.

The stats: 54.9km in 4h (w/o breaks), 14km/h average, 1200m elevation gain.
