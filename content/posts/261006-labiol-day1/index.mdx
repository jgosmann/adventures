Today is the start of a rock climbing trip together with Elena to La Mussara in
Spain. This location was a good fit because I’m at a conference in Barcelona
later. Baggage drop off and security check at the airport are quick and
uneventful for me (except that they double checked a package of cookies for
whatever reason). I board the airplane on time, but then it doesn’t depart.
Apparently, the ground crew unloaded the airplane and left without loading it
with the new luggage. But with a delay of about 20 minutes we can take off.

I meet Elena at the baggage claim in Barcelona. Our flights had almost the same
schedule, but were operated by different airlines. Somehow I couldn’t get
another seat in her flight after she booked. Once we’ve collected our luggage, we
search for the office of the car rental company to pick up our rental. It
happens to have a quite nice teal color. Getting out of the narrow parking
garage is the first challenge, but soon we are on the highway heading towards
Reus.

In the last larger village before entering the mountains, we stop at a grocery
store to get some food as we are not sure how the catering at our accommodations
will look like. Then, we drive up a winding road into the mountains to L’Albiol.
There is a small youth hostel-like accommodation, where we are the only guests,
which makes it quite comfortable. After unloading our luggage, we take a walk
through the very small village up to the hill top with the ruins of a castle.
There is some restoration work in progress and you’re not allowed to enter
officially, but as climbers we can enter the castle anyways. Unfortunately, it
is quite foggy and we don’t get a view. Back from the walk, a tasty vegetarian
paella awaits us.
